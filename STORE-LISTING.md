### Full description — 4000 char limit
```
Nova Go is a bike ride-hailing service built for Karachi.

See your fare before you book. No meter, no surge pricing, no negotiating at
the roadside. The price you agree to is the price you pay at the end of the
trip — even if traffic makes the journey longer than expected.

WHY PEOPLE USE NOVA GO

Fixed fare, shown up front
The fare is calculated from the road distance and locked when you confirm.
You can see exactly how it was worked out: a base fare plus a rate per
kilometre. If your rider asks for more than the app showed, that is not our
fare — tell our operations desk.

Riders you can check
Every rider's CNIC and driving licence is verified against the original
document by a person before they can accept a single trip. Before you get on
you can see their name, their rating, how many trips they have completed, and
the number plate you should be looking for.

A person watching every ride
Nova Go runs during hours when a real operations desk is staffed. If nobody
accepts your request, a person picks it up and places it by hand. You are told
when that happens.

Safety built in, not buried
One tap calls emergency services and alerts our desk with your live location.
Share your trip with anyone by link so they can follow it in a browser. Before
you get on, a short checklist prompts you to confirm the number plate.

Pay in cash
No cards, no wallet to top up, no minimum balance. Pay your rider at the end
of the trip. The app tells you the exact amount and warns you in advance if
your note is likely to be too large for your rider to change.

MORE THAN RIDES

Send a parcel — documents, keys, a forgotten laptop — across the city on the
same bikes, at the same fixed fare. The person receiving it can pay cash.

Ask a rider to buy something and bring it to you: medicine, groceries, a part
from the market. They send you the receipt and you pay at your door.

IN ENGLISH AND URDU

The whole app works in Urdu, right to left, including addresses and fares.

WHERE WE OPERATE

All of Karachi — Saddar to Malir, Gulshan to Baldia, North Karachi to Sea
View. Bookings are taken during staffed hours; the app tells you when we are
open.

Support: <support@novagorides.com>
Privacy: <https://novagorides.com/privacy>
Terms: <https://novagorides.com/terms>
```

### Full description
```
Earn on your own motorcycle, on your own hours, across Karachi.

YOU KEEP 85% OF EVERY FARE

The passenger pays you in cash at the end of every trip. You keep it. Nova
Go's 15% is settled separately — you are never waiting on us to be paid.

You always see the fare before you accept. No mystery jobs, no accepting
blind.

WHAT YOU NEED

A motorcycle, a valid driving licence, your CNIC, and a smartphone. Upload
your documents in the app and a person reviews them — usually the same day.

HOW IT WORKS

Tap Go Online when you want to work and Go Offline when you don't. There are
no shifts and no minimum hours. Job offers come with the fare, the distance
and the pickup, and you have fifteen seconds to decide.

BUILT AROUND HOW YOU ACTUALLY WORK

Your earnings, day by day, with this week set against last week.
Progress toward weekly bonuses shown as you go, not calculated in arrears.
A screen that tells you exactly why you are not receiving jobs, so you are not
phoning the office to ask.
Clear warnings well before unsettled commission would pause your account, and
the account details to clear it in a tap.

WE BACK YOU UP

A real operations desk is staffed during booking hours. If something goes
wrong on a trip, someone answers. Every trip is recorded end to end, so a
dispute about what happened is settled with a record rather than an argument.

IN ENGLISH AND URDU

Support: <support@novagorides.com>
Privacy: <https://novagorides.com/privacy>
Rider agreement: <https://novagorides.com/driver-terms>
```

## Notes before you submit

- **Do not claim 24/7.** The app deliberately runs staffed hours; a listing
  that says otherwise contradicts the product and the runbook.
- **Do not claim food delivery.** Food is built and switched off. The listing
  above mentions rides, parcels and errands only, which is what is live.
- **Screenshots must be real UI.** See `screenshots/README.md`.
- Both apps need the **same** privacy policy URL, reachable without login.
